Support

Hajimu is a simple gym log: record your sets, see your progress and keep your training history.

Need help or found a bug?

Write to ferarvi@protonmail.com, or use Send feedback in the app's Settings. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened; a screenshot helps a lot.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. Hajimu has no sign-up or login. Your data stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, syncs through your own iCloud account.

Does it work offline?
Yes. You can log workouts without a connection. iCloud sync catches up when you're back online.

My data isn't syncing between devices.
Make sure both devices are signed in with the same Apple ID and that iCloud is allowed for Hajimu in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. In Hajimu, open Settings and tap Sync now; the iCloud sync card shows any problem.

Can I log in pounds or machine levels?
Yes. Tap the weight title above the number to switch between kg, lb and level. Progress charts convert pounds to kilograms so you can compare sessions.

How do I delete a set or a session?
Swipe it to the left and tap Delete.

How do I back up my data?
In Settings › Backup & export, tap JSON backup to save all your sessions to a file, or CSV to open them in a spreadsheet. Import JSON backup brings a backup back in without deleting anything.
