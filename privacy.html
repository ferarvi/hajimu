Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

In short: Hajimu does not collect any personal data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your workout sessions (exercises, sets, weights, reps, notes and how the session felt) and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. We cannot see or access them.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Hajimu syncs your sessions and settings to the private database of your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they appear on your other devices. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple ID and is not accessible to us. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. You can delete it at any time in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Feedback

Send feedback opens a form hosted by Tally. Only what you choose to write in it is sent, and only when you submit it. Tally's handling of form data is described in Tally's Privacy Policy.

Files you export

Backups (JSON) and exports (CSV) are created only when you ask for them, and go wherever you choose to send or save them.

Children

Hajimu does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
